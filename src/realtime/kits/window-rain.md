# Keyframe

16:9, at least 1280×720. No people. No readable text. No album covers, no labels, no band names.

A dark room at night, one lamp, rain on a wide window. The glass is the left and center of the frame so the rain can sit on it. The rest of the room stays dim.

The bed is sixteen minutes of morning rain recorded with binaural microphones in a window on Cardener Street, Barcelona, 7 July 2014, released by _blank on radio aporee. Two other long rains are in `materials/sfx/loops/` if the picture is outdoors instead: a twenty-minute midnight rain in Serrinha do Alambari by Felix Blume, and thirty-two even minutes of late-summer rain in Fairbanks by Sean Dowgray.

Run `realtime regions scene.yaml` and fit the rain rectangle to the glass, and the lamp center to the bulb.
