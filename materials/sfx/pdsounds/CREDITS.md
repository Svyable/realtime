# PDSounds environmental reservoir

Source: [Category:PDsounds.org](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:PDsounds.org) on Wikimedia Commons.
The category holds 592 ogg files. Commons marks every one of them Public domain (checked 2026-10-11).
PDSounds.org released these recordings into the public domain. Attribution is not required. The recordist name is the one Commons stored.

This folder is the environmental slice: weather, water, nature, streets, trains, and a few interior tones.
Speech, sung music, and close-miked foley (doors, dishes, voices) were left on Commons.
Audition a file before it goes under a piano. A few town recordings have distant traffic or a murmur of people.
