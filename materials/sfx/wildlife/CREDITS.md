# Night wildlife

Checked 2026-10-12. Public Domain Mark means the recordist labeled the recording public domain on radio aporee or the Internet Archive. The loon is a U.S. government work from the Yellowstone sound library.

These are the northeastern spring nights: peepers, wood frogs, leopard frogs, tree frogs, and loons. None of the peeper files were recorded in New Hampshire. The species and the season are the same chorus.

Summer insects already in the nature library are a Pacific night (Laguna Chacahua, Mexico) and a field road (Poznan, Poland). The field cricket added here is Salt Lake City. Do not lay peepers under crickets. In New Hampshire those are different months.

Maine and Catskill peeper recordings that are CC-BY or CC-BY-SA were left where they are.
